# Market decision: one Europe, country by country

**Short answer to "all of Europe at once?": impossible for exam specifics,
shared for everything else.** EU Directive 2006/126/EC harmonises licence
*categories* and mutual recognition, but every member state runs its own
national theory exam (own bank, own language regime, own pass mark). Signs are
largely shared via the 1968 Vienna Convention (+ 1971 European Agreement).

## Architecture: common core + country packs

- **Vienna-core pack** (ships with everything): sign/signal/marking geometry
  per the Convention annexes, priority principles, generic defensive-driving.
  Redrawn SVG, no vendor pixels.
- **Country pack** (e.g. `ro`, `de`, `uk`): exam format (RO: 26Q/30min/min-22;
  DE: Fragenkatalog-based, exam offered in 12 languages incl. English),
  national deviations (speed limits, alcohol, right-of-way quirks), original
  EN (+local-language) questions mapped to chapters, `lawValidThrough` stamp.

## Launch order (decided)

1. **RO first** — we hold a 2,340-question syllabus map; EN + RO text from
   day one (serves foreigners preparing in Romania and Romanians abroad
   refreshing in English).
2. **DE second** — official Fragenkatalog is public-structured and the exam
   runs in English, so an EN-first pack is exam-faithful, not a translation.
3. UK/others by demand. The abroad scenario is covered by DE/UK packs +
   Vienna core, not by stretching RO content.

## Question policy (copyright firewall)

Original questions only, aligned to public law (national code + Vienna
annexes + EU directive). Recovered bank = chapter/weight map. Explanations
cite the article, never the vendor. English traffic terminology seeded from
official EN exam regimes (DE EN-translation wording, UK Highway Code) and
flagged for native-speaker review before v1.
